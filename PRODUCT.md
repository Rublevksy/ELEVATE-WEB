## Users

Czech and European business owners and companies seeking: a new premium digital presence, a redesign of an existing website, an e-commerce solution, a custom web application, a mobile application, or optimization of an existing digital product.

## Product Purpose

ELEVATE-WEB is the marketing and portfolio site for ELEVATE, a premium digital studio based in Prague. Its job is to immediately establish ELEVATE as premium, technically strong, and design-focused; communicate capability through the site's own visual execution; showcase selected work; and convert serious potential clients into inquiries.

## Positioning

ELEVATE combines strategy, UX/UI design, development, and optimization within one accountable team. The claim is not generic websites — it is polished digital products where visual quality, technology, performance, and business objectives work together under one team, not a fragmented vendor chain.

## Operating Context

ELEVATE serves businesses across the Czech Republic and Europe. Client engagements span: new premium websites, website redesigns, e-commerce stores, custom web applications, mobile applications, and optimization of existing digital products.
